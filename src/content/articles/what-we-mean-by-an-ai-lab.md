An “AI lab” can mean many things: a funded research institute, a product company, a classroom, or a marketing phrase. For **Bhavya AI Lab**, it means something specific and limited.

## A digital laboratory first

The first product is a **digital presence** — a place to learn in public, publish real work, and document experiments honestly. A physical space may exist later. We do not claim facilities we do not have.

## Four practices, not a slogan

We organize work around four connected practices:

1. **Learn** — practical AI education before hype
2. **Build** — projects with problem, approach, result, and lessons
3. **Experiment** — tests that record what worked **and** what failed
4. **Apply** — AI aimed at real constraints, including contexts outside major metros

These are practices, not separate products.

## Evidence over claims

Portfolio items appear only when they are real. Empty sections are preferred to invented projects, partnerships, or metrics. Status labels — Real, Prototype, Experiment, Concept, Planned — exist so visitors can see what kind of work they are looking at.

## Place without performance

The lab originates from **Himachal Pradesh**. That is context and responsibility, not a tourism brand. Serious AI work should not be limited to a handful of cities. We start where we are and document what we actually do.

## What this is not

It is not a generic AI SaaS template, a coaching institute brochure, or a claim of institutional scale we have not earned. It is an early laboratory that publishes carefully.

If you are learning or building with constrained hardware, limited bandwidth, or outside a metro hub — you are part of the audience this lab is for.
